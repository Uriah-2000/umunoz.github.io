Lesson 4 Summary Emails are the most popular form of communication in modern times, it is beneficial as it reduces the waste of paper, able to attach files of varying formats digitally, stored and organized, can be archived, searched and importantly accessed. Through the activities on the subject one was focused on the most popular email service Gmail. It is a free email service for users through the Google ecosystem. The features of the service include Spam filtering as to keeps a users information safe from hackers or alike, Built in chat, call phone as you can call anyone in the world who has an account and conversation view which allows for a conversation in a group which makes it easier for senders to communicate and gmail organizes this for convenience. Learning more in-depth about this makes this service usable for anybody in schools or business as everyone likes the idea of convenience. The second activity introduced me to the terminology of what parts of sending an email means, CC stands for Carbon Copy as you are sending the same information to the person, BCC is a Blank Carbon Copy which is sent to other receipts emails without seeing the other's email address without the primary recipient knowing. Those were the main two terms that I wanted to learn and truly understand what they mean and how they function. Upon viewing the PowerPoint, it is not just Gmail, there is Yahoo! Mail and Outlook wich are known as web based email services. What surprised me was there were browser email services like Mozilla Thunderbird, Windows Live Mail, Opera Mail but they unfortunately lack the extras that Gmail has and are mainly used by small businesses. Upon looking through the PowerPoint it occurred to me there’s a lot more about emails that I am learning about for the first time and that doesn’t cross people's minds where they really think about it. Seeing what goes on behind scenes when a message is sent is eye opening. Understanding a Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) as it goes through a server and is the standard for sending emails from device to device and is part of the TCP/IP suite. Also learning a Message Transfer Agent (MTA) which helps routes and delivers and receives messages through the SMTP, Mail Delivery Agent (MDA) which receives the messages delivered to its destination to be seen by its user. Using emails is a daily thing for everybody, especially in the graphic design field where communication is 100% required learning about how it operates and how many bells and whistles some services provide can benefit communication and business to their fullest and using the features the respective email service provides to benefit my needs.
